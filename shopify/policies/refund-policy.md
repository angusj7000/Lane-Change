# Returns & Refund Policy — draft

> Paste into **Settings → Policies → Refund policy**. Replace every **[CONFIRM: …]** and delete these notes. Note: under the Australian Consumer Law you can't exclude refunds or replacements for faulty items, and a "no refunds" policy is not allowed. The wording below keeps those rights intact.

## Change of mind

We accept change-of-mind returns within **[CONFIRM: 14/30]** days of delivery for full-price items that are unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags and packaging. **[CONFIRM: choose — refund to the original payment method / store credit / exchange only.]** Return shipping for change-of-mind returns is paid by the customer **[CONFIRM]**.

**Not eligible for change-of-mind returns:** **[CONFIRM: e.g. sale items, gift cards, items marked final sale]**.

## Exchanges

Need a different size? Email us within **[CONFIRM: 14/30]** days of delivery. We'll exchange for another size of the same tee while stock lasts. Collection 001 stock is limited, so we can't guarantee every size will be available.

## Faulty or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or wrong, contact us within **[CONFIRM: 7]** days of delivery with your order number and photos. We'll arrange a replacement, repair or refund in line with the Australian Consumer Law and cover return shipping. Natural variation in tone and texture from the garment wash is part of the finish and isn't a fault.

## How to return

1. Email **[CONFIRM: support email]** with your order number, the item, and the reason for the return.
2. We'll reply with the return address and instructions. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.
3. Pack the item securely and use a tracked service. We aren't responsible for returns lost in transit.

## Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, refunds are processed within **[CONFIRM: 5]** business days to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. Original shipping costs are **[CONFIRM: not refunded, unless the item was faulty]**.
