# Store policies

| Policy | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Shipping** | Use `shipping-policy.md`: fill every [CONFIRM] and paste into **Settings → Policies → Shipping policy**. Make sure the rates match **Settings → Shipping and delivery**. |
| **Refund / Returns** | Use `refund-policy.md` the same way, in **Settings → Policies → Refund policy**. Then set up **Settings → Policies → Return rules** to match. |
| **Privacy** | In **Settings → Policies → Privacy policy**, click **Create from template** (Shopify keeps it legally current), then check the contact details. |
| **Terms of Service** | Same: **Create from template** under Terms of service, then add your business name, ABN and contact address. |
| **Contact information** | **Settings → Policies → Contact information**: legal business name, ABN, address, email and phone. Required at checkout. |

The theme footer and product pages link to these policies automatically once they're saved.
